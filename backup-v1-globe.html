The Engineer Behind NumenX

I’ve always been passionate about building technology that solves real-world problems. My interests lie in embedded systems, FPGA design, hardware development, signal processing and product engineering.

I started NumenX with one goal: to create a company that delivers practical, reliable and innovative engineering solutions, transforming ideas into real products.

One of our greatest strengths is the engineering community we’ve built, a strong network of engineers, researchers and industry professionals, so every project benefits from the right expertise. Every project is a long-term partnership built on trust, technical excellence and innovation.

Vision

To become one of India’s leading deep-tech engineering companies, delivering world-class embedded, FPGA, automation and defense technologies.

Mission

Deliver innovative, reliable and scalable engineering solutions while bridging the gap between research and industry.
